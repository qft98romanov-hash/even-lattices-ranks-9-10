THEOREM
Let L be a positive definite even integral lattice of rank 9, with every
nonzero vector having squared norm at least 6. If its Gram determinant is
21870, then L is integrally isometric to the displayed lattice K9 in the
released certificate. In conjunction with the lower-bound theorem, K9 is
therefore the unique minimizer of e_9(6), up to isometry.

Here 'determinant' means the determinant of a Gram matrix, not the covolume;
'minimum' means squared norm. Equivalence is G = U^T K9 U with U in GL_9(Z).
The theorem classifies lattices, not their primitive embeddings in dimension48.

1. WHY THE OLD STRICT-INEQUALITY SEARCH DOES NOT SUFFICE BY ITSELF

The lower-bound search assumed det L <=21868, since rank9 even determinants
are even. At equality det L=21870 the Hermite windows change. If L_r is a
successive kernel of a shortest nonzero dual vector and d_r=det L_r, then

 d_{r-1}=d_r min(L_r^*),
 d_{r-1} <= gamma_r d_r^{(r-1)/r}.

The bound gamma9<=2^{8/7} gives

 floor(2^{8/7} 21870^{8/9}) =15910,

certified without floating point by

 15910^63 <= 2^72*21870^56 <15911^63.

For an even integral lattice of even rank2m, the determinant is congruent
modulo4 to 0 or (-1)^m. Consequently rank8 determinant15909 IS allowed;
rounding directly to15908 would be incorrect. Successive exact upper bounds:

2. THE NEW ENDPOINTS ARE EMPTY

We re-executed the exact one-vector extension generator at each new endpoint.
An extension of an even parent J is J+Z(y+z), with y in J*, z perpendicular
J, s=||z||^2>0, and class a=y+J. Write

 mu(a)=min{||u||^2:u in a},   det K=det J*s.

Using a shortest representative of a, evenness is exactly

 s+mu(a) in 2Z.

The extension has minimum at least6 exactly when the parent does and

 m^2*s+mu(m*a)>=6   for every nonzero integer m.

Only positive m with m^2*s<6 need be tested. In particular, no zero-class
layer at the order of a may be silently omitted. To be a step of the chosen
flag, the perpendicular dual vector must additionally be shortest; the code
checks this by exact enumeration in the child dual. The necessary preliminary
bound s>=3/(4 min(J*)) permits safe parent pruning.

All discriminant tables are certified complete by containing det J distinct
fingerprints after a full rational ellipsoid enumeration. Enumeration uses
exact rational LDL decomposition and exact integer coordinate endpoints.
Once all classes have representatives within the enumerated ball, their
recorded minima are exact.

5. ALL1920 E8 CLASSES GIVE THE SAME LATTICE

Let G8 be the leading8x8 block of K9 and C=G8/3. The eight basis vectors
have squared norm2 for C. Their root reflections have integral matrices

 R_i=I-e_i*(row_i C),    R_i^T G8 R_i=G8,    det R_i=-1.

On an integer pairing column p in J* they act by

 p'_j=p_j-p_i*C_ij.

A breadth-first orbit enumeration begins at the pairing column p from K9.
All orbit steps preserve the exact class minimum8/3. The computed orbit
has1920 classes, precisely the full set of admissible E8 classes. The
output stores the reflection matrices and an orbit spanning tree.

This proves equivalence of extensions: a parent isometry transports the
glue vector; replacing that transported vector by another representative
of the same class changes it by an element of J. This replacement is an
integral triangular change of basis in the extension. Since s is fixed,
no further continuous extension parameter remains. Thus every E8-based
admissible extension is isometric to K9.

No unproved assertion that the chosen reflections generate the full Weyl
group is needed: the explicitly generated subgroup is already transitive.

7. ADDITIONAL INDEPENDENT VERIFICATION

A second checker, check_equality_independent.py in the independent_review
folder, imports none of the original lattice search code. It rechecks the
new boundary exclusions by a different method, using its own rational
matrix inversion and determinant routines.

For a parent Gram matrix G, put d=det G, A=adj(G). A dual class with pairing
column p is encoded by c=A p modulo d. The discriminant quadratic form can
be encoded by Q(c)=p^T A p modulo2d. It is well defined because G is even:
replacing p by p+Gz changes this numerator by2d p^T z+d z^T Gz, a multiple
of2d. The group is generated by the columns of A, and its values are
computed without finding shortest vectors by the recurrence

 Q(c+A e_i)=Q(c)+2c_i+A_ii  (mod2d).

A hypothetical even extension of determinant T requires

 Q(c)=-T (mod2d),

since T=d*n-p^T A p and n is even. Breadth-first enumeration covers exactly
d classes. Failure of this congruence for every class rules out the
extension regardless of its minimum.

Alternatively an explicit nonzero integer pairing p proves the lifting
obstruction whenever

 4*T*(p^T A p)<3*d^2.

Indeed p determines a dual vector of squared norm(p^T A p)/d, and the
lifting inequality applies to every such nonzero dual vector. This does
not require determining the shortest dual vector.

Across the262 parent/endpoint pairs (118+138+6), the independent checker
excludes188 by explicit dual witnesses and74 by complete finite quadratic
module enumeration. Thus the new boundary exclusions do not rely solely
on repeating the original short-vector enumeration kernel.
