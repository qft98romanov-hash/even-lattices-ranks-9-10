RANK-NINE DETERMINANT GAP ABOVE THE UNIQUE MINIMIZER

Additional imported input: the upper bound gamma_9<=2 for the Hermite
constant of all real Euclidean rank9 lattices. This input is distinct from

The parent report must supply the verified public source for this input.
All statements below explicitly depend on it.

PROPOSITION
Every positive definite even integral rank9 lattice L with squared minimum
at least6 and Gram determinant at most24450 is isometric to K9 and has
determinant21870. Consequently every such lattice not isometric to K9
has determinant at least24452.

This is an excluded interval, not a claim that determinant24452 is attained.

PROOF
Write D=det L. The published lower-bound certificate already establishes
D>=21870. Take the primitive kernel J of a shortest nonzero vector in L*.
The Hermite bound gamma9<=2 gives

 det J <=2 D^(8/9).

Exact integer arithmetic verifies

 2^9 *24450^8 <15912^9.

Thus det J<15912. An even integral rank8 lattice has determinant congruent
to0 or1 modulo4, so det J<=15909. The equality addendum has established
completeness in precisely this rank8 window, including the changed lower
rank windows4648 and9494 and the newly allowed endpoint15909. Its empty
endpoint certificates are therefore directly applicable; no search at
ranks5--8 needs to be assumed complete beyond its proved domain.

The old six-branch analysis rules out all rank8 parent types other than
sqrt(3)E8,J14580,J15309a for a rank9 determinant at most24450: their
alternative branch lower bounds are at least32076,36504,39930,42282,
or the branch has no valid continuation in the window. The case det J=6561
is sqrt(3)E8 by the rank8 Hermite equality theorem.

For each of these three parents, exact discriminant-class minima were
recomputed. Any extension of squared minimum at least6 can be written
using a shortest representative of its glue class, with last diagonal
n=s+mu(a) an even integer at least6. Moreover

 det J*(8-max mu)>24450

for all three parents. Therefore n can only equal6. For every discriminant
class a we computed

 s=6-mu(a),  D=det J*s,

and tested every positive m with m^2*s<6 against

 m^2*s+mu(m*a)>=6.

The complete result within21870<=D<=24450 is

 parent          number of admissible classes      determinant
 sqrt(3)E8                    1920                     21870
 J14580                          1                     21870
 J15309a                         2                     21870.

These are exactly the classes already proved isometric to K9. This proves
the proposition. Since an even lattice of rank9 has even Gram determinant,
the next value not excluded by this argument is24452.

For comparison, reusing only the original rank8 window<=15908 would give
the weaker exclusion through24444. The slightly stronger24450 threshold
uses the explicitly verified endpoint addendum and the modulo4 restriction.

The first determinant produced by the single-layer formula above the
searched interval is26244 for the E8 parent and33534 for either other
parent. These observations do not prove the global absence of determinants
between24452 and26242, because the present shortest-dual flag windows do
not cover that larger interval.
