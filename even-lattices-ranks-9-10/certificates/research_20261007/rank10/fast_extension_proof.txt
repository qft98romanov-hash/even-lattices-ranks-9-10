Exact fast extension kernel.
Assume the parent is positive definite, even integral, and has minimum
at least6. Let its Gram G have determinant d, adjugate A and inverse A/d. For
pairing column p and new even diagonal n, put M=p^T A p and D=nd-M.
Then the child's Schur complement is s=D/d. Its dual norm at (q,m) is
  q^T G^{-1}q + (m-p^T G^{-1}q)^2/s.
The new perpendicular dual vector has norm 1/s=d/D. For q=0 no nonzero
integer m has smaller norm. For q!=0, minimizing over m gives distance
to the nearest integer. Thus the normal is shortest iff
  D (q^T A q) + dist(p^T A q,d Z)^2 >= d^2
for every nonzero q with D(q^T A q)<d^2. All terms are integers.
A single complete parent-dual list through d/D_low covers these q for
all candidate D>=D_low. The lifting lower bound permits increasing
D_low to ceil(3d/(4 min(parent*))). Negating q makes the same test, so
one representative of each +/-pair suffices.

Discriminant class fingerprints are A p modulo d. Their minima M_a are
integers with actual squared norm M_a/d. Complete enumeration through a
bound which covers all d classes proves every class minimum exactly.
The original all-layer minimum condition becomes
  m^2 D + M_(ma) >= 6d,
for each m>0 with m^2D<6d. This includes nonzero zero-class layers.
The even diagonal range and determinant congruence are tested exactly.

The parent flag basis condition min(parent*)=det(leading block)/d is
independently checked by exact dual enumeration for every parent used.
No floating arithmetic occurs. The vector enumerator is the archived
rational exact kernel; this is an optimized extension implementation,
not a fully independent implementation of vector enumeration.
