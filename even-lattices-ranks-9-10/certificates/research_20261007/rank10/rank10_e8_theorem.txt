RANK-TEN LATTICES CONTAINING sqrt(3) E8: EXACT OPTIMUM AND TWO EQUALITY TYPES
Confidential research note, 7 October 2026.

Scope. This note does NOT prove global e_10(6)=54675. It completely solves
the subclass containing a copy of sqrt(3)E8, without assuming that an
intermediate rank-nine section has the smallest possible determinant.
It also exhibits an equality lattice not isometric to the source K10.
No claim of bibliographic novelty or ambient rank48 embedding is made.

THEOREM. Let L be a positive definite even integral lattice of rank 10 and
minimum squared norm at least 6. If L contains J isometric to sqrt(3)E8,
then det(L)>=54675. Equality holds for exactly two isometry classes.
Both have minimum 6 and 294 oriented vectors of norm 6. One is the original
K10 of the supplied manuscript; the other has the Gram block form below.

1. Exact finite input.
Write Gram(J)=3C, where C is the displayed unimodular E8 root Gram matrix
in rank10_e8_certificate.json. J*/J is E8/3E8. Enumeration of all E8 vectors
of squared norm at most 8 covers all 3^8=6561 residue classes. Their exact
minimum norm distribution in E8 coordinates is
  norm 0:1; norm 2:240; norm 4:2160; norm 6:2240; norm 8:1920.
The complete vector shell counts through norm 8 are
  0:1; 2:240; 4:2160; 6:6720; 8:17520.
Consequently rho_glue(J)^2=8/3. The script uses rational LDL enumeration
with exact integer interval endpoints, and verifies every residue occurs.
Every norm outside the enumeration is >8, so these class minima are exact.

2. Automatic primitivity.
The saturation Jbar=L intersect span_R(J) is integral and lies in J*.
If Jbar/J were nonzero, choose a nonzero class represented by y in Jbar.
Subtracting a vector of J produces a nonzero vector of Jbar of squared
norm at most 8/3, contradicting min(L)>=6. Thus J is primitive.

3. Projected binary lattice and determinant bound.
Let P be the orthogonal projection of L to span_R(J)^perp. Because J is
primitive, P is a rank-two lattice and
  det(L)=det(J) det(P)=6561 det(P).
For each nonzero z in P, a lift y+z in L has y in J*. Subtract an element
of J to minimize the norm of its projection class. Then
  6 <= ||z||^2 + mu_J(y+J) <= ||z||^2+8/3,
so min(P)>=10/3.

For completeness, binary Hermite reduction gives
  det(P)>=3/4 min(P)^2.
Indeed choose a shortest primitive vector z1, extend it to a basis,
reduce z2 by z1 so |<z1,z2>|<=||z1||^2/2, and use ||z2||^2>=||z1||^2.
Thus det(P)>=3/4*(10/3)^2=25/3 and det(L)>=54675.
Equality forces a basis with projected Gram matrix
  [[10/3,5/3],[5/3,10/3]],
after changing a sign if necessary.

4. All equality cases reduce to pairs of deep residue classes.
Let a,b in E8/3E8 be the projection classes of lifts of that acute basis.
The three shortest projected directions z1,z2,z1-z2 all have squared
norm 10/3. The minimum-six condition forces the minima of the three
classes a,b,a-b to be exactly 8/3, i.e. their unscaled class minima are 8.
Conversely any such pair determines an even integral extension of
minimum six: choose norm-eight representatives u,v of a,b; the full
Gram matrix is
  [[3C, Cu, Cv],
   [u^T C,6, (u^TCv+5)/3],
   [v^T C,(u^TCv+5)/3,6]].
The off-diagonal entry is an integer because all three class norms are
8 modulo 6: u^TCv is 1 modulo 3. All diagonals are even. The six shortest
projected directions have total minimum 6. Every other nonzero projected
vector has squared norm at least 10, and vectors in J have minimum 6.
Its determinant is 54675.

Changing representatives by 3E8 corresponds to adding J vectors to the
two lifts, hence does not change the isometry class. An E8 isometry
acting on the pair of residue classes also extends to an isometry of
the constructed rank-ten lattice. Thus orbit computations on the finite
set are sufficient to classify the extensions (surjectively; distinct
orbits could a priori give isometric full lattices, addressed below).

5. Exact reflection orbit computation.
The certificate enumerates all 240 roots of C, takes one of each +/-pair,
and verifies all 120 integral reflection matrices R satisfy
  R^T C R=C, det(R)=-1.
These act on all 1920 deep residue classes transitively.
Fix the class a of w=(-1,-1,2,-1,0,0,1,1), corresponding to the original K9.
There are exactly 561 deep b such that a-b is deep. Among the 120 root
reflections, exactly 36 fix a. The subgroup generated by those 36 has
exactly two orbits on the 561 allowed b:
  one orbit of size 560, containing the original K10 class b;
  the singleton b=-a.
It is unnecessary to assert that this subgroup is the full stabilizer.
Its two orbits show that at most two full-lattice isometry types occur.
Both occur, and the nonisometry check in step 6 separates them, so there
are exactly two. This also covers cases where different E8 embeddings
could relate the lattices: the Gram ranks modulo 3 distinguish the full
abstract lattices without reference to their E8 embeddings.

6. Explicit representatives and nonisometry.
Use the original rank-nine matrix K9=[[G8,p],[p^T,6]], with
 p=(-2,1,3,-1,1,0,1,-1)^T.
The original K10 has Gram
 [[G8,p,q],[p^T,6,2],[q^T,2,6]],
 q=(1,-1,1,0,-1,-2,-2,1)^T.
The second type, temporarily denoted K10_alt, has Gram
 [[G8,p,-p],[p^T,6,-1],[-p^T,-1,6]].
Both are explicitly positive definite, even integral, determinant 54675,
and have no nonzero vectors of norm below 6. Their short shell counts are
                    norm6    norm8    norm10
 original K10         294      216       756
 K10_alt              294      216       762.
Moreover rank_F3(Gram(K10))=4 whereas rank_F3(Gram(K10_alt))=3.
Integral isometry preserves Gram rank modulo 3, so they are not isometric.
The two discriminant groups can also be read off from the determinant
54675=3^7*5^2 and ranks modulo 3 and 5 (both have rank 8 modulo 5):
 original: (Z/3)^4 x Z/15 x Z/45;
 alternative: (Z/3)^5 x (Z/15)^2.

7. Consequences and limits.
A counterexample to global e10(6)=54675 must contain no sqrt(3)E8 at all.
This is a larger exclusion than merely ruling out extensions of the
particular determinant-minimal K9. It does not exclude rank-ten lattices
whose every rank-eight section is different from sqrt(3)E8.
The alternative is an abstract lattice. A primitive embedding into a
particular extremal even unimodular lattice of rank 48 must be supplied
separately before using it as an ambient sphere-packing cross-section.

8. Reproduction.
  python verify_rank10_e8_classification.py
Required adjacent stdlib-only modules:
  exact_lattice.py (copied unchanged from the existing audited package),
  verify_rank9_extremal_example_exact.py (contains the existing K9 matrix).
The script writes rank10_e8_certificate.json and ends with
  RANK10_E8_SUBCLASS_TWO_EQUALITY_TYPES_EXACT_OK.
The finite counts above were actually computed, not inferred from a hash.
The arithmetic kernel is reused, not an independent full enumeration
implementation. The conceptual rank-two determinant argument is separate
from the archived rank-nine lower-bound search.

9. A simple gluing construction of the alternative.
The alternative is an index-three even overlattice of
  sqrt(3)E8 perpendicular sqrt(5)A2.
The nonzero gluing classes have minimum squared norms 8/3 and 10/3,
respectively, so their total is 6. In the displayed A10 basis, write w
for C^{-1}p. The orthogonal A2 basis consists of coordinate vectors
  (0,...,0,1,1), (-w,1,-2),
whose Gram matrix is [[10,-5],[-5,10]]. The direct-sum determinant is
6561*75 and dividing by the squared gluing index 9 gives 54675.
The original K10 has an orthogonal sqrt(15)A2 with Gram[[30,15],[15,30]]
and gluing index 9. These complement matrices are checked exactly by the
certificate. This description is intrinsic to the displayed E8 embeddings;
the Gram rank modulo 3 remains the proof of abstract nonisometry.

10. All deepest K9 discriminant classes.
The same classification gives an exact list of all K9*/K9 classes of
minimum 7/2. There are 561: 560 of order six and one of order two. Modulo
simultaneous negation there are 281. Explicit pairing columns are exported
by export_k9_deep_classes.py to k9_deep_class_pairings.json.
For each allowed E8 residue b, choose a norm-eight representative v; with
w=C^{-1}p use the rank-nine integral pairing column
  r=(Cv,(w^T C v+5)/3).
The last entry is integral; r^T K9^{-1}r=7/2, and the 561 adjugate residue
fingerprints are distinct. Negation corresponds to b -> a-b. Its only
fixed point is b=-a, the order-two class.
Completeness: any class of minimum 7/2 gives the even rank-ten extension
with Schur complement 5/2 and new diagonal 6. It has minimum six because
nonzero layers |m|>=2 contribute >=10 in the perpendicular direction.
Its determinant is 54675 and it contains E8, so the hexagonal equality
classification applies. Choose the acute projected basis extending the
fixed first vector. Changing the second generator by a sign, an integer
multiple of the first generator, and an E8 vector is exactly the freedom
of class representatives and negation. It produces one of the 561 columns
above. Thus the list exhausts the minimum-7/2 classes, not merely a subset.
