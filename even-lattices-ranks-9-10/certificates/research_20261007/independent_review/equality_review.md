## Why the alternate endpoint certificate is sound

Let G be the even parent Gram matrix, d its determinant, and A its adjugate. An integral pairing column p represents a dual class, encoded by `c=A p modulo d`. The map from dual classes to these fingerprints is injective: equal fingerprints imply `G^{-1}(p-p')` is integral. It therefore has exactly d classes.

The quantity `Q(c)=p^T A p modulo 2d` is well defined. Replacing p by `p+Gz` changes the numerator by `2d p^T z + d z^T Gz`, which is divisible by 2d because G is even. A breadth-first enumeration generated by the columns of A computes all d classes using

`Q(c+A e_i) = Q(c) + 2 c_i + A_ii (mod 2d)`.

An even one-vector extension has final diagonal n even and determinant `T=d n-p^T A p`, so it requires `Q(c)=-T (mod 2d)`. Absence of this residue rules out the extension without any shortest-vector calculation. The checker verifies class count d and path consistency as well as absence of the target residue.

The alternative certificate supplies a nonzero pairing p with `4T(p^T A p)<3d^2`. Its parent-dual squared norm is q=`(p^T A p)/d`, and an extension in the chosen shortest-dual flag must satisfy `s>=3/(4q)`, where `s=T/d`. To see this, lift this nonzero parent-dual vector to a child-dual vector with perpendicular coefficient of absolute value at most 1/2; its norm is at most `q+1/(4s)`. The perpendicular dual vector is shortest and has norm `1/s`, giving the stated inequality. Thus the strict displayed inequality is an explicit obstruction. It does not require proving that the supplied parent-dual vector is shortest.

These two alternate certificates are complete for the supplied 262 boundary parents; neither calls the original rational ellipsoid enumerator.

## Why the orbit gives actual integral isometries

Write `E(p,6)=[[G,p],[p^T,6]]`. Suppose an orbit edge uses an involutive parent reflection R, going from representative p0 to another representative p of the class of `R^T p0`. Set

`t=G^{-1}(R^T p-p0)` and `U=[[R,t],[0,1]]`.

Class equality and `R^2=I` imply t is integral. The checker verifies this directly. Since all these representatives have the same dual squared norm 8/3 and the perpendicular squared norm is fixed, exact matrix multiplication gives

`U^T E(p0,6) U = E(p,6)`.

This identity was checked independently for every tree edge. The determinant of U is -1. Hence each of the 1920 admissible E8-based extensions is integrally isometric to the root representative. No claim that the eight chosen reflections generate the full automorphism group is needed.
