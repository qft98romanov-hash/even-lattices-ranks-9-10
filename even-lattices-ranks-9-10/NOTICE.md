# Draft status and attribution

This is a repository preparation package dated 26 October 2026. It contains
an English working manuscript, exact-arithmetic computational evidence, and
instructions for reproduction. External scientific review and a definitive
novelty assessment remain pending.

The original motivating manuscript, *Optimal Extensions of Cross-Sections:
Sphere Packings in Dimensions 38 to 43*, is by Ivan Dorofeev, Xiaoming Sun,
and Chengu Wang. It is retained as supplied in `certificates/source_main4.pdf`.
The current research draft has been prepared by Ivan Dorofeev with AI
assistance; a final author list for a standalone publication has not been
fixed by this preparation step.

No repository-wide open-source or Creative Commons license has been assigned.
Existing attribution and any notices in the source materials are preserved.
Choose a license and finalize authorship with the relevant contributors before
making a public release.

The `certificates/` tree is a byte-preserved historical evidence bundle.
Its dated reports and status records describe their respective dates, and
some older open questions were resolved by later records. The current
English manuscript and `docs/RESULTS_AND_SCOPE.md` explain the present scope.

Historical Russian originals are retained for provenance. English counterparts
cover the mathematics; `docs/ENGLISH_READING_MAP.md` identifies them.
