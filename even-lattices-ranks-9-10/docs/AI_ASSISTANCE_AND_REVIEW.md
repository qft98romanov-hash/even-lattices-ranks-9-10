# AI assistance and review status

This package was prepared for Ivan Dorofeev through an interactive research workflow involving ChatGPT. AI assistance was used in mathematical exploration, exposition, translation, programming, certificate generation, and internal checks. The supplied evidence makes the substantive claims inspectable without relying on the authority of an AI-generated response.

## What is available for review

- Precise statements, definitions, mathematical reductions, and proofs in the English manuscript.
- Exact integer and rational verification code, explicit matrices, finite tables, and saved search records.
- Separately implemented checks for several important components, including equality-boundary exclusions, chain linkage, and intrinsic structure.
- Recorded executions and file hashes, with distinctions between static checking, fresh generation, and complete regeneration.
- Explicit external assumptions and clear limits on the packing and embedding conclusions.

“Independent implementation” in the project means that a check uses a separate implementation or mathematical procedure for the stated component. It does not mean that the entire result has been independently refereed by an external mathematician, reviewed by an external AI service, or formalized in a proof assistant.

## Current review status

The retained 8 October 2026 evidence records successful execution of the 21 routine checks and the earlier full strict-search and ambient-fiber replays. It also records a fresh generation of the new rank-ten equality jobs. The source package distinguishes this from a second optional full equality replay, which is not claimed to have been completed in that record.

The full routine suite was rerun successfully during repository preparation on 26 October 2026, using CPython 3.12.14 on Linux. No optional full regeneration or hosted GitHub Actions execution is claimed for that packaging run.

External expert validation and a definitive assessment of novelty remain pending. In particular, the rank-nine attaining lattice is a known perfect-form similarity class, $Q_{129}$; identifying it does not by itself settle priority for the global even-integral determinant theorem.

The final scholarly assessment must examine the completeness reductions, the use of external Hermite bounds, the scope of each finite enumeration, and the relation to prior literature. Passing checks and matching hashes are evidence for specific steps, rather than substitutes for that assessment.
