# Citation and source attribution

The present manuscript is a working draft prepared by Ivan Dorofeev. A final
author list for its standalone publication has not been fixed by repository
preparation. No DOI, journal acceptance, arXiv identifier, or public repository
URL is assigned here.

The motivating manuscript is:

Ivan Dorofeev, Xiaoming Sun, and Chengu Wang,
*Optimal Extensions of Cross-Sections: Sphere Packings in Dimensions 38 to 43*,
unpublished manuscript supplied by the authors.

It is included as [source_main4.pdf](../certificates/source_main4.pdf). The
current paper's bibliography gives the mathematical sources and distinguishes
their results from the new claims in the draft. In particular, the bound
`gamma_9 <= 2` is an external input, and the rank-nine minimizer belongs to
the previously known perfect form type Q129.

Add a finalized `CITATION.cff` when the authors, title, release URL and version
are agreed. This package avoids generating a citation that would imply
unconfirmed coauthorship or publication status.
